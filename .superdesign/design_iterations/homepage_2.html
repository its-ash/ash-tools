Frequently asked

Is my data really never uploaded anywhere?

Correct. Every tool processes files using in-browser JavaScript, WebAssembly, or the File API. Nothing is sent to a server — you can verify this by checking the network tab in devtools.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts, no logins, and no email capture anywhere in the toolkit.

Is Ash Tools free to use?

Yes, every tool is free and unlimited. No paywalls, no usage caps, no watermarks.

Can I use it offline?

Most tools work fully offline once the page has loaded, since all logic ships to your browser upfront.

Stop uploading your files.

Every tool loads instantly and keeps your files on your machine. No catch, no tracking.
